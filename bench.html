How the Bench decides
  • A scale can be out by half its smallest step. To keep that error under about 5%, each tinter must weigh at least ten steps: 10 g on a 1 g scale, 1 g on a 0.1 g scale.
  • Anything lighter is converted to millilitres using the density printed on the tin, for a syringe. Below 0.10 ml a 1 ml syringe is not accurate enough either.
  • Tinters are thick and some stays in the syringe. Use the same technique every time.
  • Most van scales read to 1 g, so that is the starting setting. Change it if yours reads finer.
  • Syringe sizes and the 0.10 ml limit are working assumptions until we confirm what technicians carry.
  • The example recipe uses invented numbers. It is not a real formulation.

Vans carry either the old or the new tint system. Which one these eleven belong to is still to be confirmed. Codes, material numbers and densities are from the tin labels.
