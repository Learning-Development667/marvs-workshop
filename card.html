Reference card

A strip of known greys that goes in the photo, so the app can see what the camera has done to the colours.

Print it

  1. Print this page on a colour printer, at 100% or "actual size", on plain matt paper. Glossy paper reflects light and spoils the reading.
  2. Cut out a card. There are three on the sheet.
  3. On the job, lay it flat beside the damage, in the same light, and take the photo close up.
  4. In Marv's Photo Match, press "The printed card is in this photo", then tap the dark square at each end of the card.

This is a test card. Office printers do not print greys exactly, so readings are approximate. A production card is printed professionally and each grey measured, then those values go into the app.
