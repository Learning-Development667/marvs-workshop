Reference card

A card of known greys and colours that goes in the photo, so the app can see what the camera has done to the colours. The greys correct brightness and white balance; the colours correct colour shifts and check how well the correction worked.

Print it

  1. Print this page on a colour printer, at 100% or "actual size", on plain matt paper. Glossy paper reflects light and spoils the reading.
  2. Cut out a card. There are three on the sheet.
  3. On the job, lay it flat beside the damage, in the same light, and take the photo close up.
  4. In Marv's Photo Match, press "The printed card is in this photo", then tap the dark square at each end of the card.

For a professional printer, or to print later. The PDF prints at true size and has a second page listing every patch's colour for the printer to match and measure.

Printer error carries into every reading. The app expects each patch to be exactly the colour it was designed to be. Unless the card is printed by a professional, colour-managed printer, it will not be: office and home printers are off by a noticeable amount, and that error is added to every corrected colour. For real use, have the card printed professionally and every patch measured with a spectrophotometer, then those measured values go into the app.

Why three cards? One card is a strip about 17 cm by 5 cm, so three fit on one sheet. You only need one in each photo; the others are spares, for a second van or for when one gets dirty or creased.
