Suggested mix

Van tints in parts. Mix them into the binder as usual.

A starting point, not a formulation. It comes from estimated tint colours, so check it on a test dab and adjust by eye.

Difference is measured on the paint industry's scale. Under 1 cannot be seen, 1 to 2 is a match to a trained eye, and over 5 is plainly a different colour.
